The Educated Mind

A Curated Reading Program in Ideas and Society

This list is designed for a broadly educated mind. It aims to build wide-ranging but coherent understanding of major ideas, traditions, and human questions, while remaining efficient and worthwhile. It favors foundational and globally representative works that repay careful study.

Its books span philosophy, religion, history, politics, economics, sociology, and language.

Literature, technology, natural science, mathematics, visual art, and music are deliberately excluded to keep the focus on ideas and society.

The Educated Mind

A Reading Program in Ideas and Society

A reading program for building a broad, coherent understanding of the ideas, traditions, and questions that have shaped human societies. It favors foundational, globally representative works that repay careful study, and it keeps the program lean: the longer a book is, the more it must justify its place.

The program spans philosophy, religion, historical thought and turning points, political thought, economics, and the study of society and culture. Literature, linguistics, science, mathematics, technology, art, and music are deliberately left out to keep the focus on ideas and society.

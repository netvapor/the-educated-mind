The Educated Mind

A Curated Reading Program in Ideas and Imagination

This list is designed for a broadly educated mind. It aims to build wide-ranging but coherent understanding of major ideas, traditions, and human questions, while remaining efficient and worthwhile. It favors foundational and globally representative works that repay careful study.

It comprises 130 books — 50 in world literature and narrative imagination, and 80 in Ideas & Society, spanning philosophy, religion, history, politics, economics, society, and language.

Technology, natural science, mathematics, visual art, and music are deliberately excluded to keep the focus on ideas, society, and the literary imagination.
